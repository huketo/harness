# Open beta PRD

Status: approved
Last updated: 2026-09-28

- The open beta is planned to start on 2026-11-02 for Korea only. Korean is the only UI language.
- The beta is free. There is no payment, escrow, or fee of any kind. The print-sale marketplace is planned after the beta and depends on a payment-provider contract that is not signed.
- In scope: roll posting, stock tags, follows, lab reviews, search by stock and camera.
- Out of scope for the beta: print sales, automatic posting to other social networks, a native mobile app.
- Members must be 14 or older.
- There is no product analytics SDK; beta measurement uses database counts and a post-signup survey.
