# Filmlog product context

Status: approved
Last updated: 2026-09-20

Filmlog is a community where film photographers in Korea post scanned rolls, tag camera and film stock, and follow each other. Fans browse by film stock and camera.

- **Roll**: a set of scans from one film roll, posted together.
- **Stock tag**: the film stock used for a roll, chosen from a curated list.
- **Lab review**: a member's review of a film development lab.
