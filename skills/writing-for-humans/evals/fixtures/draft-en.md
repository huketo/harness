# Supercharging Our CI: More Than Just a Speed Boost

In today's fast-paced engineering landscape, CI speed isn't just a nice-to-have — it's mission-critical. Our team embarked on a journey to transform our pipeline, and the results speak for themselves.

At its core, this wasn't about shaving minutes. It was about unlocking developer velocity. By leveraging a remote build cache, median pipeline time dropped from 24 minutes to 9 minutes. Let that sink in.

Here's the thing: caching isn't magic. It's not just a tool; it's a mindset. We navigated a complex landscape of cache keys, flaky tests, and stakeholder alignment — ultimately fostering a culture of continuous improvement.

**Key Takeaways:**
- 🚀 **Faster Feedback:** Developers get results in record time.
- 🔒 **Reliability:** Builds are now more robust and dependable.
- 🌱 **Growth:** The team is empowered to innovate.

Honestly? We're just getting started. The future of CI is bright.
