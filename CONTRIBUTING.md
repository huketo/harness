# Contribution policy

Harness is a personal showcase maintained only for its owner. This repository does **not** accept:

- bug reports or feature requests;
- issues, pull requests, patches, or other contributions;
- support requests;
- vulnerability or security reports, public or private.

There is no response or service-level commitment. Please do not send credentials, private data, or sensitive findings through repository, social, or personal-contact channels.

You may fork, use, modify, and redistribute Harness under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Maintain your fork independently. Third-party and adopted components retain their own copyright notices and license terms.
